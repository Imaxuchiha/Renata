What are the days of the week in Brazilian Portuguese?

The days of the week in Brazilian Portuguese are domingo, segunda-feira, terça-feira, quarta-feira, quinta-feira, sexta-feira and sábado. They are not the same as European Portuguese, which uses different names. This guide gives you the Brazilian forms, the correct accents, and how to use them in real sentences.

By Renata · November 20, 2026 · 5 min

The seven days in Brazilian Portuguese

Brazilian Portuguese uses a simple system: Sunday and Saturday have their own names, and Monday to Friday are numbered from segunda (second) to sexta (sixth). The word feira comes from an old word for a working day, so segunda-feira literally means 'second working day'. In everyday speech, Brazilians often drop the -feira and just say segunda, terça, quarta, quinta, sexta.

Notice the accents: terça, sábado and domingo have no accent, but you must write terça with the cedilla (ç) and sábado with the acute accent on the first a. Missing accents is a common spelling mistake among learners.

  • domingo, Sunday
  • segunda-feira, Monday (often just segunda)
  • terça-feira, Tuesday (often just terça)
  • quarta-feira, Wednesday (often just quarta)
  • quinta-feira, Thursday (often just quinta)
  • sexta-feira, Friday (often just sexta)
  • sábado, Saturday

Why Brazilian Portuguese is different from European Portuguese

In Portugal, the days are completely different: segunda-feira is still used, but Tuesday is terça-feira, Wednesday is quarta-feira, and so on, actually, European Portuguese uses the same numbered system for Monday to Friday. The real difference is that European Portuguese often uses the names feira, and the pronunciation and usage differ. However, the key point for learners is that Brazilian Portuguese uses the forms above, and they are what you will hear in Brazil.

A common mistake is to use European Portuguese forms like 'terça' in Brazil, but that is actually correct in Brazil too. The bigger issue is that some online resources mix the two. For example, European Portuguese uses 'fim de semana' for weekend, which is also used in Brazil. So the days themselves are similar, but the accent and some expressions differ. Always check that your source is Brazilian.

How to use the days in sentences

Days of the week are masculine in Brazilian Portuguese, so you say 'o domingo', 'o sábado', but for Monday to Friday you usually use 'a segunda', 'a terça', etc., because feira is feminine. When saying 'on Monday', use 'na segunda' or 'na segunda-feira'. For Sunday and Saturday, use 'no domingo' and 'no sábado'.

Here are some useful patterns: 'Hoje é segunda-feira.' (Today is Monday.) 'A reunião é na quarta.' (The meeting is on Wednesday.) 'Eu trabalho de segunda a sexta.' (I work from Monday to Friday.) 'No fim de semana, eu descanso.' (On the weekend, I rest.)

Pronunciation tips for English speakers

Brazilian Portuguese pronunciation can be tricky. Here are approximations: domingo = do-MEEN-goo; segunda = se-GOON-dah; terça = TEHR-sah; quarta = KWAR-tah; quinta = KEEN-tah; sexta = SESH-tah; sábado = SAH-bah-doo. Note that final 'o' often sounds like 'oo' in Brazil.

The 'r' in terça and quarta is pronounced like an 'h' in many Brazilian accents, especially in Rio. So terça might sound like 'TEH-sah'. The 'ç' is always a soft 's' sound. Practice these slowly and listen to native speakers.

Common mistakes to avoid

Learners often forget the hyphen in segunda-feira, but it is required. Also, don't capitalize days of the week in the middle of a sentence, in Portuguese, they are lowercase. Another mistake is to use 'em' instead of 'na' or 'no': say 'na segunda', not 'em segunda'.

Also, be careful with 'fim de semana' (weekend). It is masculine: 'o fim de semana'. And 'sábado' and 'domingo' are masculine, so 'o sábado', 'o domingo'. But when you say 'on Saturday', it's 'no sábado'.

Common questions

What is the difference between 'segunda' and 'segunda-feira'?

'Segunda' is the short form of 'segunda-feira'. Both mean Monday. In everyday conversation, Brazilians often drop '-feira', but in writing, especially formal writing, 'segunda-feira' is more common.

Are the days of the week capitalized in Brazilian Portuguese?

No, days of the week are not capitalized in Brazilian Portuguese, except at the beginning of a sentence. So you write 'hoje é segunda-feira', not 'hoje é Segunda-feira'.

How do I say 'on Monday' in Brazilian Portuguese?

Use 'na segunda' or 'na segunda-feira'. For example, 'A reunião é na segunda' means 'The meeting is on Monday'. For Saturday and Sunday, use 'no sábado' and 'no domingo'.

Is 'terça' the same in Brazil and Portugal?

Yes, 'terça-feira' is used in both Brazil and Portugal. However, European Portuguese sometimes uses different expressions and pronunciation. The days themselves are similar, but always check that your learning material is Brazilian if you want to speak like a Brazilian.

What is the weekend called in Brazilian Portuguese?

The weekend is 'o fim de semana' (masculine). For example, 'No fim de semana, eu descanso' means 'On the weekend, I rest'.
