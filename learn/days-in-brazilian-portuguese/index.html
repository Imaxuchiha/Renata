How Do You Say the Days of the Week in Brazilian Portuguese?

The days of the week in Brazilian Portuguese are: segunda-feira (Monday), terça-feira (Tuesday), quarta-feira (Wednesday), quinta-feira (Thursday), sexta-feira (Friday), sábado (Saturday), and domingo (Sunday). They are all masculine and are normally written with a hyphen. This guide explains the forms, the pronunciation, and how Brazilians actually use them in everyday speech.

By Renata · November 23, 2026 · 4 min

The Full List and How to Pronounce It

In Brazilian Portuguese, the weekdays from Monday to Friday end in -feira, which literally means 'fair' or 'market day' but functions as part of the day name. Saturday and Sunday are different: sábado and domingo. All seven are masculine, so you say o sábado, o domingo, and so on.

The accent on sábado is essential: it is SÁ-ba-do, with the stress on the first syllable. Terça has a cedilla (ç) and is pronounced 'TER-sa'. Quarta, quinta, and sexta are straightforward. Segunda is 'se-GOON-da'.

  • segunda-feira, Monday
  • terça-feira, Tuesday
  • quarta-feira, Wednesday
  • quinta-feira, Thursday
  • sexta-feira, Friday
  • sábado, Saturday
  • domingo, Sunday

Brazilian vs European Portuguese: What Changes

In European Portuguese, the days of the week are often written without -feira in everyday speech: segunda, terça, quarta, quinta, sexta. In Brazil, the full form with -feira is standard in writing and very common in speech, especially in more formal contexts. However, Brazilians also shorten them in casual conversation, so you will hear 'segunda' and 'sexta' a lot.

One important difference: in Brazil, the week is often considered to start on Sunday, while in business contexts Monday is the first working day. Also, 'fim de semana' (weekend) is the same in both countries, but the pronunciation of 'fim' in Brazil is 'feen' with a nasal sound.

Using Days in Real Sentences

To say 'on Monday' you use 'na segunda-feira' or just 'na segunda'. For example: 'Eu trabalho na segunda-feira' (I work on Monday). For 'every Monday', use 'toda segunda-feira' or 'às segundas-feiras'. Note that 'toda' agrees with the feminine 'feira', so it is 'toda segunda-feira'.

For weekend days, use 'no sábado' and 'no domingo'. Example: 'No sábado, eu vou à praia' (On Saturday, I go to the beach). To ask 'What day is it today?', say 'Que dia é hoje?' The answer could be 'Hoje é quarta-feira' (Today is Wednesday).

  • na segunda-feira, on Monday
  • toda terça-feira, every Tuesday
  • no sábado, on Saturday
  • no domingo, on Sunday
  • fim de semana, weekend

Common Mistakes English Speakers Make

English speakers often forget the hyphen in segunda-feira. In Portuguese, the hyphen is required. Also, do not capitalize days of the week in the middle of a sentence: 'Eu viajo na sexta-feira' is correct, not 'na Sexta-Feira'.

Another mistake is using the preposition 'em' instead of 'na' or 'no'. You say 'na segunda' (not 'em segunda') because 'feira' is feminine. For sábado and domingo, use 'no' because they are masculine. So: 'no sábado', 'no domingo'.

How to Practice Days in Brazilian Portuguese

A good way to learn is to say the day out loud every morning: 'Hoje é segunda-feira.' Then add what you are doing: 'Hoje é segunda-feira e eu trabalho.' This builds the association between the word and your routine.

Common questions

What are the days of the week in Brazilian Portuguese?

They are: segunda-feira (Monday), terça-feira (Tuesday), quarta-feira (Wednesday), quinta-feira (Thursday), sexta-feira (Friday), sábado (Saturday), and domingo (Sunday). All are masculine and the weekdays from Monday to Friday end in -feira.

Do Brazilians say 'segunda' or 'segunda-feira'?

Both are used. In writing and formal speech, 'segunda-feira' is standard. In casual conversation, many Brazilians shorten it to 'segunda'. The same applies to terça, quarta, quinta, and sexta.

How do you say 'on Monday' in Brazilian Portuguese?

You say 'na segunda-feira' or 'na segunda'. For example: 'Eu trabalho na segunda-feira.' Use 'na' because 'feira' is feminine. For Saturday and Sunday, use 'no': 'no sábado', 'no domingo'.

Is the hyphen in segunda-feira necessary?

Yes, the hyphen is required in correct written Portuguese. Without it, the word is considered misspelled. So always write 'segunda-feira', 'terça-feira', etc.

Are the days of the week capitalized in Brazilian Portuguese?

No, days of the week are not capitalized in the middle of a sentence. They are only capitalized at the beginning of a sentence or in titles. So write 'hoje é segunda-feira', not 'hoje é Segunda-Feira'.

What is the difference between Brazilian and European Portuguese for days of the week?

The names are the same, but in European Portuguese the short forms (segunda, terça, etc.) are more common in everyday speech, while in Brazil the full forms with -feira are often used in writing and formal contexts. Also, the week is often considered to start on Sunday in Brazil, while in Portugal it typically starts on Monday.
