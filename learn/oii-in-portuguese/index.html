Brazilian Portuguese basics

What does oii mean in Portuguese?

Oii is a casual Brazilian Portuguese greeting, a stretched-out way of writing oi, which means hi. The extra i makes it sound friendly and informal, like saying hii in English. You use it with friends, on WhatsApp, or when you want to sound warm and relaxed, not in a formal email.

By Renata · November 3, 2026 · 4 min

What oii actually means

Oi is the standard informal hello in Brazilian Portuguese. Oii is the same word with an extra i, used in writing to show a longer, softer sound. It is not a different word and it does not change the meaning. Think of it as the difference between hi and hii in a text message.

In speech you would just say oi, maybe holding the vowel a little longer. The spelling oii belongs mostly to writing: WhatsApp, Instagram comments, quick messages. It signals warmth and closeness, and it is very common in Brazil.

  • Oi = hi (neutral, informal)
  • Oii = hii (friendlier, drawn out)
  • Oiii = even more playful, often between close friends

When to use oii and when not to

Use oii with friends, family, people your own age, and in casual messages. It fits perfectly at the start of a WhatsApp chat or when you run into someone you know. It is friendly but not overly familiar with strangers, so keep it for people you already have a relaxed relationship with.

Avoid oii in formal situations: job applications, emails to a boss, official letters, or when talking to someone much older in a professional context. In those cases use Olá, which is neutral and safe, or Bom dia / Boa tarde depending on the time of day.

Oii is Brazilian, not European

This is a Brazilian Portuguese page, and oii is typically Brazilian. In Portugal, people also say oi, but the stretched spelling oii is far more common in Brazil, especially in digital writing. European Portuguese tends to use Olá more often, even in informal contexts.

If you are learning European Portuguese, oii may look odd or childish. If you are learning Brazilian Portuguese, it is a normal, friendly part of everyday texting. The difference matters because many generic online answers mix the two varieties without saying so.

How to answer when someone writes oii

The simplest reply is oi or oii back, often followed by tudo bem? (all good?). That is the standard Brazilian check-in. You can also answer with tudo bem? or tudo bom? and then continue the conversation.

If you want to sound natural, do not overthink it. A short Oii, tudo bem? is enough. Brazilians do not expect a long greeting in casual messages; they expect warmth and a quick move to the point.

  • Oii, tudo bem? = Hii, all good?
  • Oi! Tudo? = Hi! Everything okay? (very casual)
  • Oii, tudo bom? = Hii, everything good?

Common mistakes with oii

The main mistake is using oii in a formal context. It is informal and slightly playful, so it can sound out of place in a job interview or a first email to a company. Another mistake is pronouncing it as two separate i sounds; in speech it is just one long i.

Also, do not confuse oii with oi in writing. Both are correct, but oii is more affectionate. If you are unsure, use oi; it is safe in almost any informal situation.

Common questions

Is oii a real word in Portuguese?

Oii is not a separate dictionary word; it is an informal spelling of oi, the Brazilian hello. The extra i shows a longer, friendlier sound. It is very common in texting and social media in Brazil.

What is the difference between oi and oii?

Oi is the standard informal hi. Oii is the same word with an extra i, used to sound warmer or more playful in writing. In speech the difference is just a slightly longer vowel.

Can I use oii in a formal email?

No. Oii is informal and should be avoided in formal emails, job applications, or professional messages. Use Olá instead, or Bom dia / Boa tarde depending on the time of day.

Is oii used in Portugal?

Oi is used in Portugal too, but the stretched spelling oii is much more typical of Brazil, especially in digital writing. European Portuguese tends to use Olá more often, even informally.

How do I reply to oii?

A simple Oii, tudo bem? works well. You can also reply with oi or olá followed by tudo bem? Brazilians usually keep greetings short and move quickly to the point.

Does oii have an accent?

No, oii has no accent. It is just the letters o, i, i. The base word oi also has no accent. Be careful not to add accents that are not there.
