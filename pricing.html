Quick answers

The money questions.

Why no free trial lesson?

Because free lessons attract comparison shoppers rather than students, and because the diagnostic is real work with something to show for it. You keep the plan either way, and it comes off your first month if you continue.

How long until I can hold a conversation?

Simple exchanges come surprisingly early — a few months. A solid A1 is around 80 to 100 hours of study, and A2 roughly the same again. Anyone quoting you less than that is selling something.

Can I cancel whenever?

Yes. No lock-in, no fee. Groups end at the end of the month you cancel in, and unused lessons in a pack stay valid for six months.

What if the group is full?

You go on a waiting list and I tell you honestly how long I expect it to take. I do not put five or six people in a four-person group: everyone would speak less for the same money.

What time are lessons?

Evenings, European time — which is also early afternoon in much of the Americas. It is a small number of hours a week, so places are limited for real.

Group or one-to-one?

Group, for most people. It costs less, and hearing three other people make the mistake you were about to make is worth more than the extra attention. Choose one-to-one if you have a deadline or an unusual goal.
