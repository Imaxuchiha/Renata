Why this happens

Four gaps, and none of them is your vocabulary.

If you can read a Portuguese newspaper but cannot follow a conversation in a café, the problem is almost never how many words you know. It is these four things.

1. Vowel reduction

European Portuguese reduces unstressed vowels so heavily that whole syllables seem to vanish and words run into each other. Brazilian Portuguese keeps them open, which is why it sounds slower and clearer to a learner. Your ear was trained on the wrong sound system, and ears retrain with structured listening.

2. The gerund habit

You say estou fazendo. Here people say estou a fazer. Both are correct Portuguese, but only one sounds native in Lisbon. This single pattern gives you away faster than your accent does, and it is a one-afternoon fix.

3. Address and register

Brazilians default to você. In Portugal tu is the everyday informal form, and você can land as distant or even curt depending on the situation. Formal contexts use the person's title or name instead. This is a social rule before it is a grammatical one, and getting it wrong is the fastest way to sound rude by accident.

4. Vocabulary swaps

Bus is autocarro, not ônibus. Train is comboio. Phone is telemóvel. Bathroom is casa de banho — and banheiro here means lifeguard. There are perhaps eighty pairs that matter, and you meet them all in your first month.

An honest note

If you are here for the CIPLE exam, I am the wrong person.

The A2 exam used for Portuguese citizenship tests European Portuguese, and Brazilian vocabulary in the written section costs marks. I am a native Brazilian speaker; that is not my exam and I will not pretend otherwise. Look for a European Portuguese teacher who specialises in it.

Worth checking first, though: nationals of Portuguese-speaking countries are generally exempt from the language requirement altogether. And since May 2026 the residency period required for naturalisation is substantially longer than it used to be, so confirm where you actually stand before paying anyone for exam preparation.

Quick answers

What people ask me first.

Why do I understand Brazilian series but not the bakery?

European Portuguese reduces unstressed vowels heavily, so syllables disappear and words run together. Brazilian Portuguese keeps them open. Your vocabulary is probably fine — your ear was trained on a different sound system.

Did I waste my time learning Brazilian Portuguese?

Not at all. It is the same language and nearly everything transfers. You need vocabulary swaps, one grammar habit, a different default form of address, and ear training. Weeks, not years.

Will you make me sound Portuguese?

No, and I would not want to. I keep my own Brazilian accent while working with Portuguese clients daily. The goal is to understand and be understood, not to erase where you learned the language.

Which variety will you teach me?

I teach Brazilian Portuguese as it is actually spoken, and I mark explicitly what changes in Portugal and in formal writing. You end up able to switch, which is more useful than picking a side.
