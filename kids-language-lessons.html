Parent questions

Frequently asked questions about kids language lessons.

Is this Brazilian Portuguese or European Portuguese?

It is Brazilian Portuguese. Renata is honest about that from the first call. For families living in Portugal, she can point out common Portugal-specific words and pronunciation differences when they matter.

Are the Chinese lessons Mandarin?

Yes. The Chinese lessons are beginner-friendly Mandarin exposure for children, focused on sounds, simple words, culture, curiosity, and confidence.

Can lessons happen online?

Yes. Online lessons are possible when that works better for the family. For younger children, Renata will discuss attention span and format with the parent first.

Which areas do you serve?

The website is focused on families in Lisbon, Cascais, Oeiras, Paço de Arcos and nearby areas, with online options for families elsewhere in Portugal.
