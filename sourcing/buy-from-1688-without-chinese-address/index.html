1688 buying guide

How to Buy from 1688 Without a Chinese Address

You can buy from 1688 without a Chinese address by using a sourcing agent or a freight forwarder who gives you a local warehouse address. The seller ships domestically to that address, then the agent or forwarder handles export and international shipping to you. You do not need to live in China, but you do need a local receiving point.

By Renata · September 30, 2026 · 6 min

Why 1688 Blocks Most Foreign Buyers

1688 is Alibaba's domestic wholesale platform. It is built for Chinese businesses, not for overseas buyers. The site is in Chinese, prices are in RMB, and most sellers only ship within mainland China. They often do not accept foreign credit cards or international payment methods.

Registration usually requires a Chinese phone number for verification. Without that, you cannot even create a buyer account. The platform assumes you have a local address, a local phone, and a local payment method. That is the first practical blocker.

  • Language: the interface and most seller messages are in Chinese.
  • Payment: many sellers only accept Alipay or local bank transfer.
  • Shipping: domestic only, not international.
  • Verification: a Chinese phone number is often needed to register.

The Real Workarounds: Agents and Forwarders

A sourcing agent is a person or company in China who buys on your behalf. They have a Chinese address, a phone number, and payment methods. You send them the 1688 product link, they order it, receive it at their warehouse, and then ship it to you internationally. Some agents also consolidate multiple orders into one shipment.

A freight forwarder is similar but focuses on shipping. They give you a Chinese warehouse address to use as your delivery address on 1688. You buy from the seller yourself (if you can register and pay), ship to the forwarder's warehouse, and they send it abroad. Some forwarders also offer buying help for an extra fee.

  • Agent: full service, buys and ships for you.
  • Forwarder: gives you a local address, you buy and ship to them.
  • Both add cost, but solve the address and payment problem.

What an Agent Is Genuinely For

An agent is not just a mailbox. A good agent communicates with the seller in Chinese, checks stock, negotiates price, and arranges domestic shipping to their warehouse. They can also inspect the goods before shipping internationally, though quality checks vary widely. Some agents offer photos or videos of the items before you pay for international shipping.

However, an agent is not a guarantee. They do not audit factories, and they cannot promise the product matches the listing. You still take the risk. An agent reduces friction, but they do not eliminate the chance of a bad supplier or a damaged shipment.

  • Communicates with sellers in Chinese.
  • Receives and consolidates your orders.
  • Can arrange international shipping.
  • Does not guarantee quality or supplier reliability.

Payment and Communication Without Chinese

Most 1688 sellers use Alipay (支付宝, Zhīfùbǎo) or WeChat Pay (微信支付, Wēixìn Zhīfù). These require a Chinese bank account or a foreign card linked to a Chinese wallet, which is hard to set up without a Chinese phone number. An agent solves this by paying the seller for you and invoicing you in your own currency.

If you try to message a seller directly, you will likely need to use Chinese. Translation apps help for simple questions, but they fail on nuance. For example, 现货 (xiànhuò) means "in stock," but 订做 (dìngzuò) means "made to order." Confusing them can delay your order by weeks.

Step-by-Step: Buying from 1688 Without a Chinese Address

First, choose an agent or forwarder. Look for one that serves your country and handles the product category you want. Ask about their fees, consolidation options, and whether they inspect goods. Second, register on 1688 if you can, or send the product links to your agent. If you cannot register, the agent can order for you.

Third, pay the agent. They will pay the seller in RMB. Fourth, the seller ships domestically to the agent's warehouse. Fifth, the agent checks the goods (if offered) and ships internationally to your address. You pay international shipping and any customs duties. The whole process can take two to six weeks depending on shipping method.

  • Choose a reliable agent or forwarder.
  • Send product links or order directly if possible.
  • Pay the agent in your currency.
  • Agent receives goods and ships to you.
  • You pay international shipping and customs.

Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Many buyers assume the agent's warehouse address is a physical shop. It is not. It is a receiving point. If you send a return there, it may be refused. Also, do not expect the agent to negotiate a lower price unless you ask. Some agents charge a commission on the product price, others a flat fee per order.

Another mistake is ignoring shipping costs. 1688 prices look low, but international shipping can double or triple the total. Always ask for a shipping quote before committing. Finally, remember that 1688 is not Alibaba. There is no Trade Assurance, and disputes are hard to win as a foreign buyer. Use an agent you trust.

  • Do not use the agent's address for returns.
  • Ask about commission or flat fees upfront.
  • Get a shipping quote before buying.
  • Understand there is no buyer protection like Alibaba.

Common questions

Can I buy from 1688 without a Chinese phone number?

No, you cannot register directly without a Chinese phone number for verification. However, you can use a sourcing agent who has a Chinese phone number and address. They will order on your behalf. You do not need your own Chinese phone number if you go through an agent.

What is the cheapest way to buy from 1688 without a Chinese address?

The cheapest way is usually to use a freight forwarder that gives you a Chinese warehouse address. You buy from the seller yourself (if you can register and pay) and ship to the forwarder. This avoids agent commission. However, if you cannot register or pay, an agent is necessary. Compare fees carefully.

Do I need to speak Chinese to buy from 1688?

Not if you use an agent. They handle communication. If you buy directly, you will need Chinese for messages and product details. Translation apps can help with simple phrases, but they often miss context. For example, 现货 (xiànhuò) means in stock, while 订做 (dìngzuò) means made to order.

Can I use my own freight forwarder with 1688?

Yes, many buyers use a freight forwarder. You ask the forwarder for their Chinese warehouse address, then use that as your delivery address on 1688. The seller ships domestically to the forwarder, who then ships internationally to you. You must still be able to pay the seller, which often requires a Chinese payment method.

What happens if the 1688 seller does not ship to my agent's address?

Most 1688 sellers ship anywhere in mainland China, so this is rarely a problem. If a seller refuses, your agent can contact them. Some sellers only ship to certain provinces, but your agent can usually find a workaround or suggest another seller.

Is it safe to buy from 1688 without a Chinese address?

It can be safe if you use a reputable agent. But 1688 offers no buyer protection like Alibaba's Trade Assurance. You bear the risk of quality issues, wrong items, or shipping damage. An agent can reduce risk by inspecting goods, but they cannot guarantee the supplier. Always start with a small test order.
