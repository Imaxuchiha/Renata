Does Alibaba Own AliExpress?

Yes, Alibaba Group owns AliExpress. Alibaba is the parent company, and AliExpress is one of its retail platforms aimed at buyers outside China. They serve different purposes: Alibaba.com is mostly for business-to-business wholesale, while AliExpress is for smaller retail orders. Knowing which one you are on changes how you buy and who you talk to.

By Renata · November 3, 2026 · 5 min

The short answer: same group, different platforms

Alibaba Group is the parent company. AliExpress is a subsidiary platform within that group, focused on cross-border retail. Alibaba.com is a separate platform under the same group, focused on wholesale and business buyers. So when you ask if Alibaba owns AliExpress, the answer is yes, but they are not the same website or the same shopping experience.

This matters because the rules, minimum order quantities, and seller communication styles differ. On Alibaba.com you often deal with factories or trading companies. On AliExpress you usually deal with smaller sellers and retail-style listings. Both are owned by Alibaba Group, but your approach should change depending on which one you use.

  • Alibaba Group = parent company
  • Alibaba.com = B2B wholesale platform
  • AliExpress = cross-border retail platform

Why the distinction matters for small importers

If you are a small importer or dropshipper, you might start on AliExpress because it is easy to place small orders. But as your volume grows, you may move to Alibaba.com to negotiate better prices and customisation. Knowing they are under the same ownership helps you understand that both are legitimate, but they serve different stages of your business.

The ownership also means that some behind-the-scenes systems, like payment protection or dispute resolution, may share similar principles. However, the day-to-day experience is different. On AliExpress you rarely speak to a person; on Alibaba.com you often chat with a sales rep. That is where language support can make a real difference.

Common confusions: Alibaba, AliExpress, and 1688

People often mix up Alibaba.com, AliExpress, and 1688. All three are connected to Alibaba Group, but 1688 is a domestic Chinese wholesale platform. It is in Chinese and aimed at buyers inside China. AliExpress is for international retail, and Alibaba.com is for international wholesale. So while they share a parent, they are not interchangeable.

If you are buying from China, you might hear suppliers mention 阿里巴巴 (Ālǐbābā) for Alibaba, 速卖通 (Sùmàitōng) for AliExpress, and 1688 for the domestic site. Knowing these names helps when you read messages or sit in on calls. It also prevents you from assuming a supplier is on the wrong platform when they are actually referring to a different one.

Common questions

Is AliExpress owned by Alibaba?

Yes, AliExpress is owned by Alibaba Group. It operates as a separate platform focused on cross-border retail, while Alibaba.com focuses on wholesale. Both are part of the same parent company.

What is the difference between Alibaba and AliExpress?

Alibaba.com is a business-to-business wholesale platform where you often deal with factories or trading companies. AliExpress is a retail platform for smaller orders, often used by individuals and dropshippers. They are owned by the same group but serve different buyers.

Is 1688 also owned by Alibaba?

Yes, 1688 is part of Alibaba Group. It is a domestic Chinese wholesale platform, mainly in Chinese and aimed at buyers inside China. It is not the same as Alibaba.com or AliExpress, which are for international buyers.

Can I use AliExpress for bulk orders?

AliExpress is generally for smaller retail orders. For bulk or custom orders, Alibaba.com is usually more suitable because you can negotiate directly with suppliers. However, some sellers operate on both platforms, so you can always ask.
