What does 1688 com português actually mean?

1688 com português means using the Chinese wholesale platform 1688 with Portuguese-language support. By Renata · October 22, 2026 · 5 min

What is 1688 and why do Portuguese speakers search for it?

1688 (阿里巴巴, Ālǐbābā) is a Chinese wholesale marketplace owned by Alibaba Group. It is aimed at the Chinese domestic market, so almost everything on it is in Chinese and prices are in yuan (人民币, rénmínbì). Many small importers and dropshippers outside China look at 1688 because it often shows lower prices than Alibaba.com or AliExpress.

Portuguese speakers search for '1688 com português' because they want to understand the site without learning Chinese. But 1688 does not offer a Portuguese interface, and most suppliers on it do not speak Portuguese. So the search is really about finding someone who can bridge that gap.

  • 1688 is a Chinese-language platform, not a Portuguese one.
  • Prices are in yuan and aimed at buyers inside China.
  • Most suppliers communicate in Chinese, not Portuguese or English.
  • You need help to read product pages and supplier messages.

Is there an official Portuguese version of 1688?

No. 1688 does not have an official Portuguese version. The site is built for Chinese buyers, and its interface, search, and messaging are in Chinese. There is no Portuguese customer service, and no Portuguese-language help centre.

Some people use browser translation to read 1688, but machine translation often fails on product specifications, technical terms, and supplier replies. It can give you a rough idea, but it is not reliable for business decisions or for negotiating with a supplier.
