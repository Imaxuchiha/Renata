Ownership, explained

Is AliExpress owned by Alibaba?

Yes, AliExpress is owned by Alibaba Group. Alibaba launched AliExpress in 2010 as its international retail marketplace, separate from Alibaba.com, which is the wholesale side. So when you buy on AliExpress, you are buying inside the Alibaba ecosystem, even though the seller is an independent Chinese company.

By Renata · November 13, 2026 · 5 min

The short answer: yes, AliExpress is part of Alibaba Group

Alibaba Group is the parent company. AliExpress is one of its retail platforms, aimed at buyers outside China. Alibaba.com is the business-to-business marketplace for wholesale and sourcing. Taobao and Tmall serve the domestic Chinese market. They are all under the same group, but they are not the same website or the same seller base.

This matters because people often say 'Alibaba' when they mean one specific platform. If you are buying single items at retail prices, you are probably on AliExpress. If you are asking for quotes, samples or bulk prices, you are probably on Alibaba.com. The ownership is shared, the use case is not.

  • AliExpress: international retail, small orders, consumer buyers.
  • Alibaba.com: wholesale, bulk quotes, business buyers.
  • Taobao and Tmall: domestic Chinese market, mostly Chinese-language.
  • All four sit under Alibaba Group.

What 'owned by Alibaba' actually changes for you

It changes the payment and dispute layer, not the seller. AliExpress handles checkout, buyer protection and disputes through its own system. The individual seller is still an independent company, often a small factory or trading company. Being inside Alibaba does not make that seller an Alibaba subsidiary.

It also means the platform rules, fees and dispute process can change at group level. When Alibaba changes something, it can affect AliExpress sellers and buyers without the seller having any say. That is normal for a marketplace, but it is worth knowing before you build a business around one channel.

Alibaba.com and AliExpress are not the same thing

The names are close, so the confusion is understandable. Alibaba.com is where you go to request a quotation, discuss MOQ (minimum order quantity) and negotiate with a supplier. AliExpress is where you go to buy one item at a listed price, often with free shipping and a tracking number.

If you are a small importer or dropshipper, you may use both. You might test a product on AliExpress, then contact the same or a similar supplier on Alibaba.com for bulk. The supplier may even be the same company, but the conversation, the pricing and the terms are different.

The Chinese names behind the platforms

Alibaba Group is 阿里巴巴 (Ālǐbābā) in Chinese. AliExpress is 全球速卖通 (Quánqiú Sùmàitōng), which literally means 'global fast-selling channel'. Alibaba.com is often just called 阿里巴巴国际站 (Ālǐbābā Guójì Zhàn), the international station.

Knowing these names helps when you read supplier messages or hear a Chinese supplier refer to the platform. A supplier might say they sell on 速卖通 (Sùmàitōng) rather than AliExpress, and it is the same thing. It is a small detail, but it saves confusion on a call.

Where the confusion comes from

Most of the confusion comes from the word 'Alibaba' being used as a shorthand for the whole group. People say 'I bought it on Alibaba' when they mean AliExpress, or 'Alibaba owns the factory' when they mean a seller on the platform. Neither is accurate.

Another source is the similar branding and the shared account system in some regions. You may log in with the same credentials, but you are still on different marketplaces with different rules. Reading the URL is the quickest way to know where you are.

How this affects buying from China in practice

If you are buying from China, the ownership question is less important than the seller question. Alibaba Group owning AliExpress does not tell you whether a specific seller is reliable. That depends on the seller's history, communication and the terms you agree.

Common questions

Is AliExpress the same company as Alibaba.com?

No, they are different platforms under the same parent, Alibaba Group. AliExpress is retail and international. Alibaba.com is wholesale and business-to-business. They share ownership but not the same sellers, pricing or terms.

Does Alibaba own the sellers on AliExpress?

No. Sellers on AliExpress are independent companies, often small factories or trading companies. Alibaba Group owns the marketplace, not the individual seller. That is why seller reliability still needs to be checked separately.

When was AliExpress launched and by whom?

Alibaba Group launched AliExpress in 2010. It was created as the group's international retail marketplace, separate from Alibaba.com. The exact corporate structure may change, but the ownership by Alibaba Group is the key point.

Does this mean AliExpress is safe to buy from?

Ownership by Alibaba Group does not guarantee any individual transaction. AliExpress has its own buyer protection and dispute process, but the seller is independent. Check the seller's history and terms before you buy, especially for larger orders.
